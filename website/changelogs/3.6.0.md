# v3.6.0 - Ask Dopamine!

### New Features
* **Ask Dopamine:** Dopamine can now reply to your messages! Simply mention it or reply to one of its messages. It can explain core features, provide you help, and do anything else that traditional AI can! The difference is that it's locally hosted on a cute little GTX 1060 (or Snapdragon 8s Gen 3, depending on what time-of-the-day you use it), doesn't chug a single drop of water, energy usage is negligible, and exactly ZERO data is stored permanently! As counter-intuitive as this may sound, this feature is meant to be a form of protest against AI. Ethically & locally hosted and efficient AI is what AI is supposed to be - not the big data centers and hundreds of GPUs chugging thousands of Watts just to reply to a simple "hello"!
* **Daily Cat:** Get a picture of (my) cute cat(s) every day! `/daily cat start` to enable.
